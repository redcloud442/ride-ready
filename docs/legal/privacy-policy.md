# RideReady Privacy Policy

_Effective: [DATE] · Draft — have a lawyer review before publishing._

RideReady ("we", "us") is operated by **Ride Ready**. Questions: **[PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]**.

RideReady helps you keep track of your vehicles: fuel and charging, trips, maintenance, costs and documents.
This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

## Summary

- We collect what you enter, plus location **only while you record a trip**.
- Receipt and odometer photos are read **on your phone**. We don't send them to an AI service.
- Your garage, trips, receipts and documents are private to your account and the family members you invite.
- No ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and we don't sell your data.
- You can delete your account in the app. That removes your data and stored files.

## What we collect

| Data | Where it comes from | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email, password (stored hashed) | You, at sign-up | Your account and sign-in |
| Name, email, profile picture from Google | Google, if you choose "Sign in with Google" | Your account and sign-in |
| Profile photo | You, optional | Shown on your profile and to family members |
| Preferences (currency, units, trips on/off) | You | Setting up the app for you |
| Vehicles (make, model, year, powertrain, color, odometer) | You | The core service |
| Fuel, charging, maintenance, expenses, battery health | You (typed in, or read from a receipt photo you confirm) | Costs, economy and reminders |
| Receipt photos, documents and manuals | You, from the camera or photo library | Attached to your records |
| Trip routes, distance and times (GPS) | Your phone's location, **only while you record a trip** | Trip distance and logs |
| Fuel and charging price reports | You, optional | Shared price list (see below) |
| Family invites (invitee's email) | You | Sharing your garage |
| Sign-in sessions: IP address, device/browser type | Your device | Keeping you signed in and protecting your account |

We do **not** collect location in the background or when no trip is recording, and we don't collect contacts,
advertising IDs, or usage analytics.

### Camera, photos and location

- **Camera:** photographing receipts and the odometer, and scanning family invite QR codes.
- **Photo library:** only the photos you choose to attach.
- **Location:** only while you record a trip you started. Trips are off by default.

Text in receipt photos is read on your device (Apple Vision on iOS, Google ML Kit on Android). The extracted
values only prefill a form, and nothing is saved until you confirm it.

## How we use it

To run the app: store your records, calculate costs, fuel economy and other statistics, show reminders,
sync between your devices, and share with family members you invite. We also use it to secure your account
(sessions, rate limiting) and to answer you when you contact us.

We don't use your data for advertising, sell it, or use it to train AI models.

## Who can see it

- **You.**
- **Family members you invite.** Anyone who accepts your invite can see and edit your whole garage: vehicles,
  records, trips with their routes, receipts and documents. You can remove them at any time.
- **Other users, for price reports only.** When you report a fuel or charging price, others see the price,
  unit, region you entered and the time. Your name and account are not shown.
- **Service providers** who host the app for us, under contract and only to provide the service:
  - [HOSTING / DATABASE PROVIDER, REGION]
  - [FILE STORAGE PROVIDER, REGION]: receipts, documents and profile photos, private and not publicly accessible
  - Google, only if you use Google sign-in
- **Authorities**, where the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete a record it is hidden immediately and kept as
a deletion marker so your other devices sync correctly. When you **delete your account**, we delete your
account, your records and trips, your stored files, and the family you own.

## Your choices and rights

- **See and edit:** everything you entered is in the app.
- **Delete:** delete single records in the app, or your whole account in Profile → Delete account.
- **Permissions:** turn off camera, photo and location access in your phone's settings at any time.
- **Other requests** (a copy of your data, objections, questions): email [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL].

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA) you may have more
rights, including access, correction, deletion, portability and complaining to your data protection
authority. Our legal bases are performing our contract with you (running the service), our legitimate
interest (security), and your consent (location, camera, photos), which you can withdraw at any time.

## Security

short-lived signed links. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at [SECURITY CONTACT EMAIL] if you find a problem.
